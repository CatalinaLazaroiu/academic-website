About me

My work brings together smart grids, electromagnetic compatibility, renewable energy integration and energy storage. I study how microgrids, university campuses and energy communities can support a resilient, decarbonised power system — and how people take part in that transition. At the Faculty of Electrical Engineering I combine teaching, research and the coordination of student internships.
